# Segment Taxonomy

## Segments

- `S1`:
  - `Name`: Multi-brand ecommerce operator
  - `Description`: Brand aggregators or holding-company operators running multiple storefronts on Amazon and expanding to Walmart. High spend volume, sophisticated on performance metrics, and sensitive to managed-service pricing. Evaluate vendors on cost-per-outcome, contract fairness, and support quality rather than feature novelty. Example account: Ejam.
  - `Primary Buyer Persona`: Head of Ecommerce or VP Digital Commerce
  - `Primary User Persona`: Centralized ecommerce manager or media buyer
  - `In-scope evidence types`: Late-stage commercial calls, pricing follow-ups, managed-service objection conversations, QBRs

- `S2`:
  - `Name`: Independent DTC brand scaling retail media
  - `Description`: Single-brand operators moving from Sponsored Ads into DSP and AMC. Less operationally mature than S1. Higher reliance on managed service support, and more likely to evaluate on ease-of-use and time-to-value. Less price-sensitive on managed service than S1, but more likely to ask for hand-holding.
  - `Primary Buyer Persona`: Growth or ecommerce lead with budget authority
  - `Primary User Persona`: Founder-adjacent or individual media buyer
  - `In-scope evidence types`: Discovery and intro calls, trust-building conversations, platform demos

- `S3`:
  - `Name`: International market entrant (China / Europe)
  - `Description`: Brands or operators entering China (JD, Alibaba ecosystem) or European markets. Primary competitive context is Pacvue or local ad tool displacement. GTM motion and proof requirements differ from domestic segments. Regulatory and naming constraints may apply.
  - `Primary Buyer Persona`: International commerce lead or regional head
  - `Primary User Persona`: Region-specific media buyer or agency partner
  - `In-scope evidence types`: Exploratory calls, competitive evaluation conversations, international-expansion planning discussions

- `S4`:
  - `Name`: Retail Media Agency
  - `Description`: Independent agencies (not holdco) managing Sponsored Ads and DSP campaigns on behalf of brand clients. Evaluate Xnurta on reporting breadth for client delivery, portfolio scalability across diverse accounts, and control depth for managing varied client objectives simultaneously. ABM target per 2026 sales strategy. Named accounts: Blue Wheel Media, Right Side Up, Awesome Dynamic, Point Story, Pitted Labs, Vinculum.
  - `Primary Buyer Persona`: Agency head, Director of Retail Media, or VP of Commerce at the agency level
  - `Primary User Persona`: Account manager or media buyer running multiple client accounts in the platform
  - `In-scope evidence types`: Agency pitch calls, platform evaluation calls focused on client reporting or portfolio management, agency-to-brand referral conversations

## Notes

- Do not collapse S1 and S2. Their managed-service sensitivity and proof requirements differ meaningfully — S1 buyers pressure price, S2 buyers pressure support quality.
- Do not collapse S4 with S1. Agency buyers (S4) buy on behalf of clients and evaluate on portfolio management and client reporting. Brand buyers (S1) evaluate on their own business outcomes and cost predictability. The proof requirements are structurally different.
- Do not use "all customers" as a segment.
- Mark segment as "unresolved" if the transcript does not provide enough context to map it cleanly.
