# Metrics Scorecard

## Core Metrics

- `Qualified pipeline creation`
- `Stage conversion rates` — F1→F2, F2→F3, F3→Close
- `Close blockers by stage` — what stops deals from advancing
- `Primary objections in qualified deals`
- `Primary proof requests in qualified deals`

## Xnurta-Specific Additions

- `Managed service objection rate in F3` — how often managed service fee appears as a primary or secondary blocker in late-stage commercial conversations; track whether this is stable, rising, or declining quarter over quarter
- `AMC fee friction frequency` — how often AMC incremental fee appears as an objection; compare against PackView AMC mention rate as a proxy for the free-alternative awareness level
- `Competitor mention rate by competitor` — track Pacvue, Intentwise, Skai, and Perpetua separately; which is most frequently named, and at which stage; use to prioritize competitive response investment
- `Category name resonance` — track buyer-language alignment with each of the four naming candidates (AI-powered retail media optimization, retail media execution intelligence, autonomous retail media management, agentic retail media platform); code organic buyer language only
- `S3 international deal stage distribution` — are international deals progressing past F1/F2, or stalling there; indicates whether the international motion is real or aspirational
- `DAA co-sell conversion rate` — compare close rates for Amazon AE-referred leads vs. inbound and event-originated leads; indicates whether the Amazon partnership is producing qualified or only warm leads
- `S4 agency pipeline as % of total pipeline` — the 2026 sales strategy targets independent agencies via ABM; track whether this segment is converting at the rate expected
- `Events pipeline contribution` — benchmark actual events-originated pipeline against the $31.6M target; track stage distribution of event-originated leads to assess evidence quality vs. awareness-only value

## Notes

- Keep the scorecard tied to questions the evidence work is expected to answer.
- Review monthly alongside the evidence review cadence defined in Epic #88 (Cross-Functional Operating Rhythm) once that work is complete.
- Do not add metrics that cannot be derived from transcript evidence or Fireflies data — if a metric requires a source system that doesn't exist yet, note it as a future dependency.
