# Strategic Questions

## Category and Positioning

- Which of the four category naming candidates lands most credibly with S1 and S2 buyers? Does "agentic" or "autonomous" resonate more than "optimization"?
- What language do buyers use unprompted when describing what Xnurta replaces or what problem it solves? Does it align with any of the four candidates?
- When buyers compare Xnurta to Pacvue, Skai, Intentwise, or Perpetua, what differentiation lands and what falls flat?

## Sales Model

- Does the ~10% of spend compensation model create friction with S1 multi-brand operators who track cost vs. perceived labor closely? Is this friction concentrated in S1 or does it appear across segments?
- Which objections in F3 conversations are pricing/commercial model problems vs. positioning/messaging problems vs. product gaps?
- How often does managed service fee become the primary blocker vs. AMC fee vs. contract language — and does the pattern differ by segment?

## Customer Advisory Board and Research Infrastructure

- Which buyer segments (S1, S2, S4) have enough trust and engagement to participate in structured research?
- The 2026 sales strategy targets top 10 accounts for 1:1 ABM — do any of these belong in a high-signal research tier (CAB)?
- What strategic questions should a CAB answer that current transcript evidence cannot resolve?

## International Expansion (S3)

- Is Pacvue displacement in China and Europe a real competitive motion based on evidence, or aspirational based on assumptions about Pacvue's presence there?
- What country-specific objections emerge in international conversations that differ from US objections?
- Does the US GTM motion (Amazon-first, DAA co-sell) translate to international markets, or does the motion need to change for EU and China channels?

## Competitive Conversion

- Which competitor (Pacvue, Skai, Intentwise, Perpetua) generates the most F2-qualified leads?
- Which competitor is most frequently cited in F3 conversations that stall — is it a pricing problem or a proof problem?
- What proof types are most frequently requested in F2 and F3 conversations when a specific competitor is named?

## Amazon Partnership Leverage

- Are DAA co-sell conversations (Amazon AE-referred leads) converting at higher rates than inbound or event-sourced leads?
- What does the AE handoff look like in Xnurta's highest-value F3 conversations — is Amazon actively participating, or has the conversation moved to Xnurta-only?
- Which Amazon proof points (Innovation Awards, AMC Hub, DAA program benefits) are most frequently cited as trust signals by buyers?

## Events as Pipeline Source

- The 2026 plan targets $31.6M in events pipeline across 121 events. What evidence from event-originated conversations is being captured, and how does it differ from inbound evidence in terms of stage and segment distribution?
- Which events or event formats produce the highest-quality evidence (specific objections, named competitors, buying criteria) vs. surface-level awareness conversations?
