# Battle Cards

Portable markdown module for workbook artifact `4.3 Battle Cards`.

## Purpose

Turn competitive narrative into usable sales strategy.

## Structure

1. `Competitor Approach & Category`
2. `Competitor Profile (Porter's Framework)`
3. `Key Purchasing Criteria (Benchmark Comparison)`
4. `How to Win (Sales Strategy)`

## Non-negotiables

- Define the competitor's approach, not just the company.
- Compare on criteria that matter to the buyer, not feature volume.
- Tie every claim to a strategic trade-off.
- Document both `When We Win` and `When We Lose`.

## Required sections

- `Competitor Approach & Category`
- `Approach Trade-off`
- `Goals & Assumptions`
- `Strengths`
- `Weaknesses`
- `Pricing Model`
- `Key Purchasing Criteria`
- `How to Win`
- `When We Win`
- `When We Lose`
- `Next Required Artifact`

## Inputs

- `Positioning Canvas`
- `Attack Matrix`
- `Eval Framework`
- `Customer Interview`
- `Elevator Pitch`
- `Segment Playbooks`
- `Competitive Intelligence Log`

## Best practices

- Use exact rep-ready language where possible.
- Treat incompleteness as a quality issue for certification later.
