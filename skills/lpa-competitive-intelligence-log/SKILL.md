# Competitive Intelligence Log

Portable markdown module for the workbook's ongoing `Competitive Intelligence Log`.

## Purpose

Prevent launch messaging from drifting away from competitive reality.

## Non-negotiables

- This is ongoing from Stage 2 through post-launch.
- Log every signal as encountered.
- Review before every competitive deal.
- Review monthly on a fixed cadence.
- When a staleness trigger fires, update downstream artifacts within one sprint.

## Required fields

- `Date`
- `Competitor`
- `Signal Type`
- `Source`
- `Signal Detail`
- `Impact Assessment`
- `Battle Card Updated?`
- `Owner`
- `Notes`

## Instructions

1. Log every competitive signal encountered in interviews, discovery, press, product pages, or internal reviews.
2. Mark whether the signal requires monitoring, a battle card update, or escalation.
3. Review before every competitive deal and on a monthly cadence.
4. Cascade relevant changes into Positioning Canvas Step 2, Attack Matrix, and Battle Cards.

## Output

- `Signal Tracker Entries`
- `Staleness Triggers`
- `Artifacts to Update`
- `Next Required Artifact`

## Handoff

Feeds:

- `Battle Cards`
- `Attack Matrix`
- `Positioning Canvas`
