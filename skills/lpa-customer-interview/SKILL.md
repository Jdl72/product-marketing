# Customer Interview

Portable markdown module for workbook artifact `2.1 Customer Interview`.

## Purpose

Gather primary evidence that validates pain, alternatives, trust requirements, and customer language.

## Inputs

- `START HERE`
- `Risk & Assumption Register`

## Use this tab in two ways

1. Read the guide once before running the first interview.
2. Copy the recording template section for each interview conducted.

## Non-negotiables

- This tab is the primary evidence engine for the playbook.
- Read upstream tabs before interviewing.
- High-priority risks and hypotheses should be tested first.
- Use exact customer wording in downstream artifacts.
- If a segment has not been interviewed, call any downstream segment narrative speculative.

## Best practices from the source materials

- Replace fictional quotes with real customer language.
- Use customer words for status quo alternatives and pain, not your own summary language.
- Interview participants are strong candidates for later bar tests.
- For mental-model interviews, do not bias the conversation by oversharing the product.
- Capture control-language verbatim when buyers use terms like guardrails, boundaries, leash, or limits.

## Instructions

1. Build the interview around the specific hypotheses and segments already defined.
2. Use customer language capture as a first-class output.
3. Record alternatives, workarounds, and trust/control expectations explicitly.
4. Score evidence quality, not just anecdotal richness.
5. Produce snapshots that can be reused in downstream narrative artifacts.
6. Push exact quotes into downstream tabs where wording matters.

## Output

- `Interview Snapshot`
- `Validated Pain Patterns`
- `Customer Language`
- `Evidence Scores`
- `Alternatives and Workarounds`
- `Hypotheses Confirmed / Weakened`
- `Next Required Artifact`
