# Customer Interview Rubric

## Purpose

Evaluate whether a completed customer interview artifact is good enough for internal PMM use and downstream evidence consumption.

Use this rubric after every interview cycle — at minimum after §2F is complete, and again when contributing to §2H synthesis.

## Scoring scale

- `1 = poor`
- `3 = acceptable`
- `5 = strong`

## Dimensions

### 1. Screener rigor

Question:

Were the right people interviewed?

High score:

- participant fits the target segment and persona defined in `START HERE`
- §2A screener criteria were applied before scheduling
- screener pass status is documented in §2B
- participant was not interviewed within the prior 90 days

Low score:

- participant was recruited opportunistically without checking screener criteria
- screener pass status is blank or marked conditional without explanation
- CRM was not checked for prior contact history

### 2. Preparation quality

Question:

Was §2C completed before the call?

High score:

- §2C contains a background summary, company context, prior product interactions, and a hypothesis-about-pain pre-write
- research brief is specific to this participant, not generic

Low score:

- §2C is blank or contains placeholder text
- the hypothesis-about-pain pre-write is missing
- the interviewer opened the call without completing preparation

### 3. Learning goal alignment

Question:

Did §2D goals map to RAR hypotheses?

High score:

- 3 explicit learning goals are written before the interview
- each goal references a specific RAR hypothesis ID
- the question guide in §2E covers the learning goals

Low score:

- learning goals are missing or written after the interview
- goals are not tied to RAR hypothesis IDs
- the interview covered topics not connected to any active hypothesis

### 4. Note quality

Question:

Are §2E notes verbatim where it matters?

High score:

- verbatim quotes are captured in the dedicated quotes column
- paraphrased content is explicitly marked `[paraphrased]`
- notes are organized by phase

Low score:

- quotes are summarized without flagging paraphrase
- the verbatim quotes column is empty
- notes are not organized by phase and cannot be reconstructed

### 5. Snapshot completeness

Question:

Does §2F have all required fields?

High score:

- Memorable Quote is present and attributed
- 3 Key Insights are present
- 2 Identified Opportunities are present
- Hypothesis Impact block is complete (Hypothesis Tested, Cumulative Evidence Rating, Confidence Shift, Evidence Summary)
- Follow-Up Actions table is filled or marked N/A

Low score:

- any required field is missing
- Memorable Quote is paraphrased without flagging
- Hypothesis Impact block is partially complete
- snapshot was not completed within 24 hours of the interview

### 6. Synthesis contribution

Question:

Does §2H reflect this interview's signal?

High score:

- interview ID appears in §2H when saturation threshold has been reached for the segment
- the interview's top insight is represented in the Recurring Pain Patterns table
- the Hypothesis Scorecard reflects updated evidence from this interview

Low score:

- interview was completed but §2H was not updated when saturation had been reached
- the interview's signal does not appear anywhere in the cross-interview synthesis
- synthesis was deferred without a documented reason

### 7. Evidence calibration

Question:

Is the Evidence Calibration Rubric applied correctly?

High score:

- Cumulative Evidence Rating in §2F matches the rubric definition (Validated requires 3+ independent sources with no contradictions)
- rating reflects cumulative evidence across all interviews, not just this one
- Falsified ratings trigger an immediate RAR update

Low score:

- rating is applied based on a single interview
- Validated is claimed with fewer than 3 independent sources
- contradictions are present but the rating is still Validated
- Falsified evidence is logged but RAR is not updated

## Pass / fail rule

Pass if:

- no §2F field is missing
- all quotes that appear in downstream artifacts are verbatim or explicitly marked `[paraphrased]`
- every Hypothesis Tested in §2F appears in the RAR
- average score is at least `3`
- `snapshot completeness` is at least `3`

Fail if:

- any §2F snapshot field is missing
- quotes are paraphrased without being flagged as `[paraphrased]`
- hypotheses tested do not appear in §2F
- evidence rating is Validated based on a single interview
- §2C was not completed before the call
- the participant failed the §2A screener and the interview was conducted anyway

## Review notes to capture every time

- strongest part of the interview
- weakest part of the interview
- screener or recruiting gap identified
- hypothesis that needs reformulation
- candidate signal for §2H synthesis
