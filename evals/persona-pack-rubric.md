# Persona Pack Rubric

## Purpose

Evaluate whether a persona pack captures a real buyer or user pattern rather than a generic profile.

## Scoring scale

- `1 = poor`
- `3 = acceptable`
- `5 = strong`

## Dimensions

### 1. Persona specificity

Question:

Is the persona concrete enough to represent a real pattern in the market?

High score:

- persona name, role title, and company context are specific
- the pack distinguishes buyer from user where relevant
- the persona is not just a demographic shell

Low score:

- the persona is generic or interchangeable
- buyer and user roles are blurred despite meaningful differences
- company context is absent or vague

### 2. Jobs, pains, and outcomes quality

Question:

Does the pack capture the real work the persona is trying to do?

High score:

- primary jobs, pains, and desired outcomes are explicit
- primary and secondary pains are separated clearly
- outcomes reflect what success means to the persona

Low score:

- the jobs are generic tasks without context
- pains and outcomes are superficial
- the pack lists symptoms without underlying goals

### 3. Buying and evaluation signal

Question:

Does the pack explain how the persona evaluates options and moves toward change?

High score:

- buying triggers, evaluation criteria, current alternatives, and objections are all present
- the evaluation logic reflects evidence rather than internal assumptions
- the pack reveals what pushes the persona to act

Low score:

- buying triggers are missing
- evaluation criteria are abstract or invented
- the pack does not explain why the persona would switch from the status quo

### 4. Language fidelity

Question:

Does the pack preserve useful customer language?

High score:

- language to use and language to avoid are grounded in evidence
- wording helps downstream messaging stay close to real buyer speech
- the pack avoids flattening signal into generic marketing language

Low score:

- language guidance is generic or slogan-heavy
- evidence-backed wording is missing
- the pack tells teams how to speak without showing why

### 5. Evidence quality and confidence

Question:

Is the persona explicit about what is observed versus inferred?

High score:

- evidence basis is present and meaningful
- confidence is calibrated to source quality and consistency
- the pack makes uncertainty visible where needed

Low score:

- evidence basis is missing
- confidence is implied rather than stated
- the pack overstates certainty about weak signal

## Pass / fail rule

Pass if:

- core identity, jobs, pains, outcomes, evaluation criteria, objections, evidence basis, and confidence are all present
- average score is at least `3`
- `evidence quality and confidence` is at least `3`

Fail if:

- the persona is missing evidence basis
- jobs, pains, and outcomes are not explicit
- buying triggers or evaluation criteria are absent
- the language guidance is unsupported by evidence

## Review notes to capture every time

- strongest persona signal
- weakest or most speculative section
- biggest evidence gap
- downstream artifact that should consume this persona next
