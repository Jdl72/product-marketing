# Content Calendar Rubric

## Purpose

Evaluate whether a content calendar reflects real PMM priorities rather than a generic publishing schedule.

## Scoring scale

- `1 = poor`
- `3 = acceptable`
- `5 = strong`

## Dimensions

### 1. PMM alignment

Question:

Do calendar entries clearly connect to a campaign goal and source artifact?

High score:

- each entry ties to a campaign goal and source artifact
- the calendar reflects PMM priorities instead of arbitrary output volume
- content choices connect back to positioning or GTM work

Low score:

- entries are disconnected from PMM strategy
- source artifacts are missing
- the calendar is just a publishing list

### 2. Audience and channel fit

Question:

Does each item match the right audience and distribution channel?

High score:

- audience is explicit
- channel choice matches the audience and asset type
- the calendar avoids one-size-fits-all content assumptions

Low score:

- audience is missing
- channel choice appears arbitrary
- asset type and channel do not fit each other

### 3. Message quality

Question:

Is the core message specific enough to guide actual content creation?

High score:

- core message is concrete and reusable
- CTA is aligned with the audience and goal
- the message is not generic filler

Low score:

- core message is vague
- CTA is absent or weak
- content direction would need to be re-derived later

### 4. Operational clarity

Question:

Can the calendar be executed without major cleanup?

High score:

- date, owner, and status are all explicit
- the calendar shows a believable operating cadence
- handoff to the execution team is clear

Low score:

- ownership is missing
- status is unclear
- timing is too vague to use

### 5. Reuse and traceability

Question:

Can the calendar be reviewed and updated without losing strategic traceability?

High score:

- entries retain clear links to source artifacts
- the calendar can be updated while preserving why each item exists
- the structure is reusable across campaigns

Low score:

- the link back to PMM source material is lost
- updates would erase strategic rationale
- the structure is too ad hoc to reuse

## Pass / fail rule

Pass if:

- date, asset type, audience, campaign goal, core message, source artifact, owner, status, distribution channel, and CTA are all present
- average score is at least `3`
- `PMM alignment` is at least `3`

Fail if:

- source artifact is missing
- owner or date is missing
- entries cannot be tied back to PMM priorities
- the calendar is operationally unusable

## Review notes to capture every time

- strongest planned item
- weakest planned item
- biggest traceability gap
- campaign area that needs more support
