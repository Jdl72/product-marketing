# Conversation Synthesis Rubric

## Purpose

Evaluate whether a synthesis of multiple conversation records is good enough for internal PMM use.

## Scoring scale

- `1 = poor`
- `3 = acceptable`
- `5 = strong`

## Dimensions

### 1. Pattern grounding

Question:

Are the major synthesis claims clearly grounded in multiple conversation records?

High score:

- recurring pains, outcomes, and objections cite underlying records
- strong claims show stronger evidence than emerging claims

Low score:

- patterns sound plausible but do not show what supports them

### 2. Citation traceability

Question:

Can a reviewer trace key synthesis statements back to specific records and quotes?

High score:

- representative quotes include record identifiers and transcript anchors
- strategic claims name the records behind them

Low score:

- synthesis forces a reviewer to guess where a pattern came from

### 3. Contradiction preservation

Question:

Does the synthesis preserve real differences or tensions across records?

High score:

- conflicting signals are named clearly
- stakeholder or segment differences are not flattened away

Low score:

- tension disappears into one smooth narrative

### 4. Scope discipline

Question:

Is the synthesis appropriately scoped for the number and type of records available?

High score:

- confidence matches the small or large size of the evidence set
- the synthesis does not overgeneralize from thin evidence

Low score:

- the synthesis sounds like market truth from only a few calls

### 5. PMM usefulness

Question:

Can a PMM use this synthesis directly for persona, positioning, or GTM work?

High score:

- outputs are specific, structured, and action-relevant

Low score:

- outputs remain generic even after synthesis

## Pass / fail rule

Pass if:

- average score is at least `3`
- `pattern grounding` is at least `3`
- `citation traceability` is at least `3`

Fail if:

- key patterns have no record-level support
- contradictions are erased
- the synthesis overclaims beyond the available evidence

## Review notes to capture every time

- strongest supported pattern
- weakest or thinnest pattern
- contradiction worth preserving
- schema gap exposed
- candidate automated eval
