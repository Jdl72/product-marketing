# PMM System Epics

## Epic 1. Market and Competitive Intelligence

Goal:

Build the research layer that helps the PMM system understand the market before producing messaging.

Jobs to be done:

- analyze the competitive landscape by segment
- run win/loss analysis
- identify unmet needs
- assess distinctive competencies

Outputs:

- competitive landscape brief
- win/loss synthesis
- unmet needs map
- distinctive competency memo

## Epic 2. Positioning and Buyer Understanding

Goal:

Build the strategy layer that turns market evidence into clear buyer understanding and positioning.

Jobs to be done:

- develop buyer and user personas
- map the buyer experience
- formulate product positioning
- generate outcome-based value propositions

Outputs:

- persona pack
- buyer journey map
- positioning brief
- value proposition brief

## Epic 3. GTM and Launch Strategy

Goal:

Build the PMM workflows that translate positioning into launch and distribution plans.

Jobs to be done:

- draft GTM plan
- propose outcome-based digital marketing strategy
- create demand-generation messaging
- integrate value propositions into existing channels

Outputs:

- GTM plan
- digital marketing strategy brief
- demand messaging pack
- channel integration plan

## Epic 4. Sales Enablement and Asset Creation

Goal:

Build the asset layer that equips sales and field teams with differentiated enablement material.

Jobs to be done:

- educate the sales channel
- build data sheets
- create white papers and ROI assets
- build problem presentations and scripts
- generate pricing materials

Outputs:

- battle cards
- solution brief
- technical data sheet
- ROI asset pack
- sales scripts
- pricing materials

## Epic 5. Ongoing Campaign Support

Goal:

Build the recurring operations layer that keeps marketing teams supported after launch.

Jobs to be done:

- provide market and product insight to outbound marketing
- maintain a content calendar
- generate recurring assets like newsletters and press releases

Outputs:

- outbound insight brief
- content calendar
- newsletter draft
- press release draft
