### `desired_outcomes`

- Get up and running quickly once the decision is made.
- Improve reporting and visibility without heavy manual effort.
- Preserve flexibility if internal teams, agencies, or operating models change.
- Tie analytics and audience insight more directly to execution and performance.

### `current_alternatives`

- `Pacvue`
- `Intentwise`
- internal dashboards and internal build efforts
- existing agency workflows
- self-service without managed service

### `buying_triggers`

- Ability to move quickly after agreement
- Strong audience-building and analytics capabilities
- Confidence in onboarding and support
- Reduced risk if the commercial or agency relationship changes later

### `evaluation_criteria`

- predictable, explainable pricing
- support quality and onboarding confidence
- advanced analytics / AMC usefulness
- audience-building strength
- workflow fit with current tools and team structure
- flexibility of contract or partnership model

### `objections`

- Managed service can feel overpriced relative to perceived labor or value.
- Analytics value alone may not be enough if execution still feels manual or fragmented.
- Buyers hesitate when product boundaries are unclear across analytics, audience creation, and campaign management.
- Contract and packaging details can block momentum even when product interest is strong.

### `trust_requirements`

- clear commercial and contract terms
- confidence that support and onboarding will actually work in practice
- honest boundaries on what is automated vs manual
- continuity if agencies or internal ownership change

### `competitors_and_substitutes`

- `Pacvue`
- `Intentwise`
- internal tools
- agency-delivered service models
- building in-house

### `language_to_use`

- "full AMC strategy"
- "get those kickstarted"
- "support is great"
- "super simple to navigate"
- "data portability"
- "audience building capabilities"

### `language_to_avoid`

- generic premium-service claims without scope clarity
- product framing that sounds like only a reporting tool
- rigid partnership language that ignores real org-change risk

### `contradictory_signals`

- In `convrec-fireflies-01KM6GSRBZNXNSKQW5BQNDG2M9`, the buyer wants strong support but resists the managed-service price model.
- In `convrec-fireflies-01KMDFR8SB2ZEPT3452AFH85F9`, the buyers value analytics and audience capabilities, but still question how much manual campaign work remains.

### `strategic_implications`

- Positioning should not lead with generic "all-in-one" claims. It should clarify where the product reduces technical and workflow burden, and where service or execution still needs to be decided.
- Messaging should separate `platform value`, `audience / analytics value`, and `managed service value` rather than bundling them into one claim.
- Commercial and transition-risk messaging may be a meaningful trust lever for agency-involved deals.

### `recommended_downstream_artifacts`

- persona pack focused on operator / agency evaluator
- positioning brief for analytics plus audience-building wedge
- objection handling asset for managed service versus self-service
